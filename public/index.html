Payslips

Basic and TADA come from the Employees tab and cannot be edited here. A month keeps the Basic and TADA it was first saved with, so later changes never alter old payslips; if an employee's Basic was 0 or wrong when you saved, fix it on the Employees tab and then use "Refresh rates from Employees" for that month. Only rows you have typed in (or that are already saved) are saved. Half day deducts 0.5 day of basic only. TADA is deducted for inactive days and LWP only. PL and Reserved are stored but do not change the salary.
